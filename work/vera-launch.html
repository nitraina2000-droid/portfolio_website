Go-to-market case study · VERA by Kamakshi

Launching VERA through the eyes of one customer

A go-to-market plan for a new luxury bag label with no sales, no reviews and no data yet.

All people shown on this page are concept visuals I made with AI.

The CEO asked me for a go-to-market plan. Instead of starting with channels and ads, I started with the woman who would buy, and planned every decision around the moments of her journey.

The CEO shared the product photos and an early version of the website. I did the research, strategy, creative direction and launch plan, using AI tools for drafts and visuals.

Follow her journey in seven moments. Each one shows what I planned for that moment.

01Segmentation & targeting

Who she is

She has three weddings, a sangeet and Diwali in the next few months, and she wants to look different at each one.

What I planned
Core

The occasion shopper

Dressing up for weddings, festivals and parties. VERA's brand line "Where Tradition Meets Luxury" and its first statement pieces were made for her, and India's wedding season alone drives around ₹6.5 lakh crore in trade.1

Second

The gift buyer

Buying for a sister, a friend or a bridesmaid. A beautiful bag is a safe, special gift.

Later

The everyday self-purchaser

Women increasingly buy luxury for themselves.2 She becomes a target as the everyday range grows.

She is a working persona built from market research, not from customer interviews.

02Positioning

What she needs to believe

She's never heard of VERA. Before she buys, three questions run through her mind.

Her three doubts
"Will it go with my outfit?"
"Is it worth the money?"
"Can I trust a brand I've never heard of?"
Positioning statement

For women dressing up for weddings, festivals and celebrations, VERA offers hand-picked statement bags from around the world, without designer-label pricing.

Where VERA sits
VERA

Hand-picked statement pieces that look special, at an accessible price

Mass bag brandsZouk, Caprese and similar.3 Affordable and everyday, with one house style many people carry.
Designer-label clutchesAnekaant, Peora and NR by Nidhi Rathi, around ₹2,700 to ₹3,400 on Nykaa Fashion.4 Special, but a known label's price.
03Awareness & promotion

She scrolls past

Most Indian shoppers now discover new products on social media, and almost all of that discovery happens on Instagram and Facebook.5 So VERA had to win her attention in the feed.

What I planned

Brand-first ads, made for her feed

At this stage she doesn't know VERA, so the ads lead with the brand and the occasion, with a soft call to action. Prices shown in the ads were placeholders.

MehendiSangeetReception

Creators, not celebrities. Micro-creators style one VERA bag across three looks, paid in product. It's a model other Indian bag brands have used to reach new buyers,6 and it answers her first doubt before she even has it.

04Consideration

She hesitates

She visits, but she isn't sure. I gave each of her doubts its own ad, so every ad has one job.

"Will it go with my outfit?"

Show one bag across Indian and western looks, so she pictures it with her own outfits.

05Conversion · Price · Place

She's ready to buy, or buying for someone else

She's decided. Now the price has to feel fair and the checkout has to feel safe.

06Product & retention

The bag arrives

The moment she opens the box decides whether she comes back, and whether she tells her friends.

The launch range
Occasion pieces
Gift edit
Everyday

Lead with occasion pieces, then a gift-ready edit and a small everyday range to grow into.

After the purchase
  • Unboxing as part of the product: considered packaging and a handwritten note
  • A simple request for a review, to build the trust a new brand lacks
  • Early access to new pieces through email and WhatsApp
07Launch roadmap

How VERA learns from her

With about 90 planned pieces and no sales history, picking a "hero" product upfront would have been a guess. So the plan lets real customers show which pieces work, then backs them.

Week 1

Measure and earn trust

Set up sales tracking, and gather the first genuine reviews through pop-ups and early buyers.

Move on whenTracking works end to end and the first reviews are live.
Weeks 2 to 3

Test the catalogue

Catalogue ads on Meta and Google show the whole range on a small budget, alongside organic Instagram.

Move on whenThere's enough data to see a baseline cost per customer.
Weeks 4 to 5

Back the best sellers

Shortlist the pieces people actually buy, and invest in creators and better content for those.

Move on whenThe best sellers return more than they cost in ads.
Week 6 onward

Scale for the season

Move the budget to proven pieces for the wedding and festive season, and build repeat buyers.

Move on whenCustomers come back, at a cost VERA can sustain.

The thinking behind it

Three key decisions

i
Instead of
Picking one hero bag to promote
I chose
Test the whole range first
Because
With no sales data, any pick is a guess. Let customers choose, then invest.
ii
Instead of
Targeting every woman who buys bags
I chose
Lead with the occasion shopper
Because
The brand story and the first pieces were made for celebrations. Everyday comes later.
iii
Instead of
Permanent markdowns to drive sales
I chose
Honest prices, planned offers
Because
Constant "% off" makes a luxury label look cheap. Value comes from service and gifting.
Reflection

What I'd take forward

I presented the plan to the CEO, who approved it. My internship ended before launch, and I don't know whether it has been put into action, so I have no results to share. After the internship, I went back to the plan, researched the market properly and rebuilt it. This page shows that rebuilt version.

  1. i
    Start with the customer, not the channel

    Planning around her moments made every choice, from pricing to packaging, easier to justify.

  2. ii
    Let data pick the winners

    With a large range and no history, testing before investing is cheaper and more reliable than backing one piece on instinct.

  3. iii
    Trust comes before scale

    A new brand with no reviews struggles to convert, so earning the first genuine reviews belongs in week one.
