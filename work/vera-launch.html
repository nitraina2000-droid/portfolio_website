A launch plan for VERA

A new luxury bag label with no sales, no reviews and no data yet. Here's how I planned its launch.

The CEO asked me for a go-to-market plan for VERA's launch. I broke it into the seven questions every launch has to answer, and made one clear decision for each.

The CEO shared the product photos and an early version of the website. I did the research, strategy, creative and launch plan, using AI tools for drafts and visuals.

01Target customer

Who should VERA sell to first?

Decision

Women shopping for weddings, festivals and parties. Gift buyers next, everyday buyers later.

First

Occasion shoppers

VERA's line "Where Tradition Meets Luxury" and its first statement pieces suit them, and the wedding season alone drives around ₹6.5 lakh crore in trade.1

Next

Gift buyers

A statement bag is an easy, special gift for a sister, friend or bridesmaid.

Later

Everyday buyers

Women increasingly buy luxury for themselves,2 which matters more as the everyday range grows.

Based on market research, not customer interviews.

02Positioning

What should VERA stand for?

Decision

Hand-picked statement bags from around the world, without designer-label pricing.

Positioning statement

For women dressing up for weddings, festivals and celebrations, VERA offers hand-picked statement bags from around the world, without designer-label pricing.

03Awareness

How will people discover VERA?

Decision

Instagram first: brand-led ads, plus micro-creators styling one bag three ways.

Most Indian shoppers discover new products on Instagram and Facebook,5 so that's where VERA starts.

MehendiSangeetReception

Creators, not celebrities. Micro-creators style one VERA bag across three looks, paid in product, a model other Indian bag brands have used to reach new buyers.6

04Consideration

Why would someone trust a new brand?

Decision

Answer the three doubts new customers have, with one ad for each.

Doubt 1
"Will it go with my outfit?"

Answer: show one clutch across every function.

Doubt 2
"Is it worth the money?"

Answer: show the detail and the care in choosing it.

Doubt 3
"Can I trust a new brand?"

Answer: remove the risk of buying.

07Launch roadmap

How should VERA launch?

Decision

Test the whole range first, then put money behind what sells.

With about 90 planned pieces and no sales history, picking a "hero" product upfront would be a guess.

Week 1

Measure and earn trust

Set up tracking and gather the first genuine reviews.

Move on whenTracking works and the first reviews are live.
Weeks 2 to 3

Test the range

Small-budget catalogue ads show every piece.

Move on whenThere's a baseline cost per customer.
Weeks 4 to 5

Back the best sellers

Invest in creators and content for what sells.

Move on whenBest sellers earn back their ad cost.
Week 6 onward

Scale for the season

Move budget to proven pieces, build repeat buyers.

Move on whenCustomers return at a sustainable cost.

Reflection

What I'd take forward

The CEO approved my plan. My internship ended before launch, so I have no results to share. Afterwards, I went back, researched the market properly and rebuilt the plan. This page shows that version.

  1. i
    Start with the customer, not the channel

    Deciding who to sell to first made every later choice easier to justify.

  2. ii
    Let data pick the winners

    Testing before investing beats backing one piece on instinct.

  3. iii
    Trust comes before scale

    A new brand with no reviews struggles to sell, so the first reviews belong in week one.
