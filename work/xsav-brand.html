Brand case study · XSAV Lab

Giving XSAV Lab one clear story

XSAV Lab is a Pune-based tech agency that offers cybersecurity, cloud engineering, AI agents and custom software. Its brand, though, only talked about security. During my internship, I defined one clear message for the company, set simple rules for how it looks and sounds, and applied them across its sales and social content.

01
The problem

One company, three different impressions

When I joined in July 2026, I read everything XSAV had published: its website, LinkedIn posts and sales documents. The same company came across differently in each place.

What it said

The website described XSAV only as a cybersecurity company, leaving out its cloud, AI and software work.

How it looked

The old logo used colours that didn't appear anywhere else on the website.

How it sounded

Words like "cutting-edge" and "leverage" made XSAV sound like any other IT company.

Before designing anything, I needed to agree on what the brand should actually say.
02
The approach

Four steps, from listening to sign-off

I framed the work around three questions: what do we say, how do we look, and how do we sound?

Step 1

Listen first

Reviewed the website, social posts, sales decks and a few competitors, and noted every inconsistency.

Step 2

Define the message

Wrote one sentence about what XSAV does, with three key messages to support it.

Step 3

Set simple rules

Turned those decisions into rules for the logo, colours, font and tone.

Step 4

Get sign-off

Presented the message and the guide to the CEO, who approved them.

Why this message

Businesses often juggle separate vendors for security, cloud and AI. XSAV's strength is doing all of it with one team, so the message had to cover everything, not just security.

03
The outcome

One message, one simple guide

Everything starts from one sentence that covers all of XSAV's work.

The one line

"We keep businesses secure, run them on the cloud and put AI agents to work for them."

Tagline: Innovate with AI. Secure with confidence.

Now on XSAV's website and in all eight sales decks. See it live on xsavlab.com ↗

05
Reflection

What I'd take forward

The work is still in use: the tagline leads XSAV's website and appears in all eight sales decks, and the material is shared with clients and new joiners.

  1. 01
    Keep it short

    A guide only works if people actually use it, so I kept it to the essentials: message, logo, colours, font and tone.

  2. 02
    Track results from day one

    I didn't track reach on the LinkedIn posts, so I can't show which ones worked best. Next time, I'd set up simple tracking before the first post goes live.

  3. 03
    Follow your own rules

    Some of my own carousels drifted from the brand colours. Consistency has to start with the person who writes the rules.
